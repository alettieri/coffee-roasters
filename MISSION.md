# Mission: Build and Understand the Coffee Roaster Platform

## Why
Build the Coffee Roaster Discovery platform as a working personal product while using it to learn Vue, Nuxt, Nitro, and Cloudflare well enough to make sound implementation and operational decisions.

## Success looks like
- Explain how a request moves through Nuxt, Nitro, Cloudflare bindings, Neon, and R2.
- Develop features locally without risking production data.
- Recognize when fast local development is sufficient and when a production-runtime preview is required.
- Diagnose whether a failure belongs to application code, local emulation, or a managed service.

## Constraints
- This is initially a single-user pet project.
- Learning value matters alongside delivery speed.
- Operational complexity should remain proportional to the project.

## Out of scope
- Becoming a general Cloudflare infrastructure specialist.
- Reproducing every managed cloud service locally.
